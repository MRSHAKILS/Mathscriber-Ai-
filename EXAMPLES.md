## 🎨 Image Quality Examples

### ✅ Good Image Qualities

**Example 1: Clear Photo**

- High resolution (1920x1080 or higher)
- Good lighting, no shadows
- Straight angle, no distortion
- Clean white background
- Black ink clearly visible

**Example 2: Scanned Document**

- 300 DPI or higher
- High contrast
- No creases or folds
- Properly aligned

**Example 3: Digital Drawing**

- Tablet drawing with stylus
- Clean lines
- Consistent thickness
- Good spacing

### ❌ Poor Image Qualities

**Example 1: Blurry Photo**

- Out of focus
- Motion blur
- Low resolution (below 800x600)

**Example 2: Poor Lighting**

- Dark shadows
- Glare or reflections
- Uneven lighting

**Example 3: Cluttered**

- Multiple equations cramped together
- Handwritten notes around equations
- Background noise/patterns

## 💡 Pro Tips from Experience

### Tip 1: Pre-process Images

Before uploading:

- Crop to just the equation
- Adjust brightness/contrast if needed
- Remove any background clutter
- Ensure image is right-side up

### Tip 2: Break Complex Equations

For long equations:

- Split into parts
- Convert each part separately
- Combine LaTeX codes manually
- Results in better accuracy

### Tip 3: Verify Output

Always check:

- Parentheses match correctly
- Fractions properly formatted
- Exponents in right place
- Greek letters correct

### Tip 4: Build Template Library

Create saved templates:

- Common equation structures
- Frequently used notation
- Standard formulas
- Quick reference

### Tip 5: Use Canvas for Speed

For simple equations:

- Drawing can be faster than photo
- More control over appearance
- Immediate feedback
- Easy to correct
