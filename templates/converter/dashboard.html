Detailed Report & Sources

This section provides the full analysis and the sources from our project proposal, outlining the market landscape and the technologies we're building upon.

Market Analysis

The market for equation-to-LaTeX conversion is dominated by a few key players, most notably Mathpix. While effective for printed text, its accuracy with varied and "messy" handwriting is inconsistent. Furthermore, its pricing model can be prohibitive for students.

On the other end of the spectrum, numerous free tools exist, but they suffer from low accuracy, distracting advertisements, and a complete lack of support for complex structures like tables. They often fail on anything but the cleanest, simplest equations.

Our Strategic Gap

Scribble-to-LaTeX is designed to fill this critical gap. By focusing our AI model (inspired by research like LaTeX-OCR and TAMER) specifically on handwritten content and tables, we can achieve superior accuracy where other tools fail. Our project proposal identified this as the single biggest pain point for students and researchers.

Our competitive advantage is threefold:

  1. Superior Handwriting Accuracy: Built from the ground up for scribbles, not just printed text.
  2. Novel Table Recognition: A key differentiator, saving hours of manual formatting.
  3. Accessible Pricing: A generous free tier and an affordable academic plan make it accessible to our core audience.

By leveraging open-source foundations (like ViT models) and focusing on a specific, high-value problem, we can outperform incumbents in our niche while remaining cost-effective.
